## What is an SSL Certificate?

An SSL certificate authenticates a website’s identity and enables HTTPS encryption. When valid, your browser shows a padlock icon. Certificates are issued by Certificate Authorities like Let’s Encrypt, DigiCert, and Cloudflare.

## Why SSL Matters

Without encryption, data on public Wi-Fi or shared networks is visible to anyone. SSL protects login pages, payment forms, and API calls. Google also uses HTTPS as a ranking signal — sites with valid certificates rank higher.

## Common SSL Issues

**Expired Certificate**: Browsers show a "Not Secure" warning. Most certificates last 90 days to 2 years.

**Domain Mismatch**: The www and non-www versions need separate certificates.

**Mixed Content**: HTTPS page loading HTTP resources triggers browser warnings.
